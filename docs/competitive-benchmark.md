# Competitive Benchmark

## Positioning statement

SmartPantry is the practical food-management app for students living independently, combining expiry-first decisions, local-first reliability, and assistant guidance in one lightweight Android flow.

## Benchmark table

| Product | Expiry alerts | AI help | Analytics | Offline-first | Pricing | Onboarding friction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SmartPantry | Yes (1-2 day reminder + reminder hour) | Yes (chat + cook-now mode) | Yes (saved/wasted KZT, used-before-expiry, trend) | Yes | Free MVP | Low (local sign up / sign in) |
| NoWaste | Yes | Partial (assistant features vary by tier) | Moderate | Partial | Freemium | Medium |
| Pantry Check | Yes | Limited | Basic | Partial | Freemium | Medium |
| SuperCook | Indirect (recipe-driven) | Recipe discovery | Limited | Mostly online-centric | Free/Freemium | Low |

## SWOT summary

- **Strengths:** local-first speed, student-focused UX, practical assistant prompts, visual + KPI analytics
- **Weaknesses:** no cloud sync yet, no barcode DB integration in MVP
- **Opportunities:** Firebase sync, barcode scanning, receipt OCR, retention nudges
- **Threats:** mature competitors with broader ecosystems and paid growth channels
